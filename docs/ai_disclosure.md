## Planet Zoo Archipelago AI Usage Disclosure

- Planet Zoo Archipelago (the client) **was built with substantial AI assistance**. 
- **The game client** (memory reading, the in-game unlock gates, the patch installer) was written largely with an AI coding assistant (Claude). AI wrote much of the code. A human directed the work, tested every feature in the live game, and reviewed every change before it was committed. Nothing goes in without a human signing off.
- **Reverse engineering** of Planet Zoo was done the same way. AI did much of the decompiler reading and analysis. Every finding was checked against the running game before anything relied on it.
- **The design is human.** What counts as an item, what counts as a check, and how progression works were decided by people, not suggested by an LLM.
- **The PopTracker pack** started from a pack-builder template. Its logic and autotracking fixes were AI-assisted and verified against a live multiworld room.
  - AI assisted in renaming the items after changing a naming scheme to match the AP world.
  - **No AI art.** The species icons in the tracker are extracted from Planet Zoo's own game files. Maps in the Poptracker were made in Excel, per hand.
- **The APWorld** was built by a newbie programmer, with AI assistance in explaining programming basics. (i.e. Why isn't this working, why is this method better than this?)
  - If I couldn't understand the code it gave me, I rewrote it, or found a different solution.
- If you find a bug, it was probably ours to catch. Please report it to us in the Archipelago Discord under future games - [Planet Zoo thread](https://discord.com/channels/731205301247803413/1326681567665324082).
